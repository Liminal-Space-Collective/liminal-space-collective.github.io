FAQ

Common questions

Do I have to be a professional artist?

No. Liminal Space membership is organized around participation and the kind of support you want, not professional status.

Can I change tiers?

Yes. The membership structure is designed so people can move between levels as their needs and level of participation change.

Do higher tiers include lower-tier benefits?

Yes. Member includes Friend benefits, Creator includes Member and Friend benefits, and Builder includes all lower-tier benefits.

Is Creative Commons still free?

Yes. Creative Commons remains part of the free Friend tier and serves as an open entry point into the Liminal Space community.

Are all partner discounts guaranteed?

No. Discounts depend on participating partners and may vary by business, service, or program period.

What counts as a Builder project?

Builder is intended for larger or more complex projects that need ongoing planning, production, fabrication, event, logistical, or project-development support.

Does Builder include unlimited production labor?

No. Builder includes coordinated project support, but substantial labor, materials, equipment, or specialized services may require a separate project budget agreed upon before work begins.
